# Sunk-Cost Decision Skill

Structure type: `comparison_frame`

Use this skill when a learner asks about sunk cost, escalation of commitment, trying to recover prior spending, or whether to keep investing time or money in a failing option.

## Teaching Contract

- Teach these terms in learner-facing copy: sunk cost, past cost, continued investment, future benefit, and opportunity cost.
- Teach in this order: separate past cost; compare future value and future cost; choose by next outcome; check decision rule.
- Use `narrative_branch` for a concrete choice, `comparison` for the next-cost versus next-benefit comparison, `classification_sort` to separate past costs from reasons that matter now, and `knowledge_check` to apply the rule once more.
- Do not use `simulation_play`, `probability`, or `system_builder` for this topic.

## Content Rules

- Past spending is unrecoverable in every branch. Do not imply that extra spending can recover it.
- The decisive comparison is only: what must be paid from now on, what can still be gained, and what alternative is being given up.
- Use familiar scenarios such as leaving a film, cancelling a project, or leaving a queue. Do not turn the lesson into a generic slider, a trend chart, or a balance dashboard.
- Each branch must visibly change the consequence. Keep copy in natural Simplified Chinese; do not use raw labels such as “入口”, “决策链”, `factor`, or `effect`.
